2026 District Select Nomination

AAU Select Tour District Select Basketball Camps are built for players who are serious about development, competition, and learning what it takes to grow in the game.

Coaches, parents, trainers, teammates, and community members can recommend players who may be a strong fit — or a player can nominate themselves.

Who we are looking for Players who show strong potential, coachability, competitiveness, work ethic, basketball IQ, and a real desire to improve. Players do not have to be finished products.
Being nominated does not guarantee a camp spot. The official invitation is emailed to the player about three hours after you submit. An invitation does not reserve a place until the player registers and payment is completed.
